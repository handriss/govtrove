For years, finding government contracts meant fighting SAM.gov's broken search or paying $6,000–$50,000/year for an enterprise tool. We think that's absurd.

Public contracts are public data. Searching them shouldn't require a training course or a procurement budget.

We built GovTrove around a simple observation: contractors don't read 50-page Statements of Work to decide if an opportunity is worth pursuing. The first pass is always the metadata — title, agency, set-aside, deadline. So we built the best metadata search tool we could, and made it accessible to everyone.

Free to use. No paywall, no gimmicks.

Compliant by Design

Small Tech, Big Impact

No investor board. No enterprise sales team. Just a better search tool for contractors who need one.

PSA: SAM.gov Registration Scams

SAM.gov registration is free. If someone is charging you hundreds of dollars to register, that's a scam. GSA has identified 400+ fraud attempts targeting small businesses since 2021. Need legitimate help registering? Find your local APEX Accelerator — they provide free assistance.
